- By traveling through Hell, Purgatory, and Heaven, Dante is showing us living mortals the fundamental structure of reality
- Intersection of metaphysics and physics

- By using both a classical figure and a modern one to explain the structure of the soul and the nature of consciousness, Dante is demonstrating the dualism inherent in this sort of metaphysical investigation. 

- Some of these things can be extrapolated through logic, derived by reason from observed experience and study alone, and Virgil brings those to him from the classical era. 

- Lombardo brings the modern second step, the question of free will in relation to the soul

- Conclusion? 
	- The other side of the nature of reality, besides reason, is faith, and that must be explained by Beatrice herself.
