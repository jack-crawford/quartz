If he was real, Sigurd could have been Arminius or Sigibert of the Franks.

Arminius, a Cherusci (Germanic) tribal leader, wiped out three Roman legions led by Quintilius Varus, in the Teutoburg forest in 9 AD,  massacring them entirely. “So unsettling was the defeat that when the news reached Rome, the emperor Augustus commanded that the city be patrolled at night to prevent an uprising. For months afterward Augustus suffered deep despair. He left his beard and hair uncut and, often striking his head against the door to his chamber, he would call out, ‘Varus, give me back my legions!’” (23). 

The argument that Sigurd is Arminius is based on the fact that his family’s names mostly began with Seg- or Segi- (meaning victory, similar to the old Norse sig-) and that Arminius is most likely a Roman name given to the leader. Additionally, Arminius destroyed legions who bore dragon banners, and Sigurd killed a dragon. That is the extent of the similarities. 

Sigibert, meanwhile, had family problems like Sigurd and his wife’s name was Brunhilda (like Brynhild in the saga). That’s about it.

Sigurd and Gudrun are the main characters, more or less. 
Sigurd is a great warrior who defeats the dragon/wyrm Fafnir and takes his treasure, even as Fafnir warned him that the treasure will be his death. Sigurd essentially says “eh, I gotta die someday so I might as well be rich”
