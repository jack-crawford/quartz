There are some fundamentally scary things in here. 
The biggest ones: 
- “Confidence in AI is associated with reduced critical thinking effort, while self-confidence is associated with increased critical thinking effort” (6.1.1).
- “In certain cases where GenAI is perceived to have a strength relative to the user’s own capability, GenAI responses are perceived to make few mistakes” (5.2.3).
	- Jesus Christ is this alarming - if you don’t know how to do something, and you get ChatGPT to do it for you, you’re disinclined to question the output because you don’t UNDERSTAND WHAT THE OUTPUT SHOULD BE!!!

This article largely focuses on productive forms of critical thinking, the critical thinking required for *outputs*, and how critical thinking in knowledge workers shifts from the manual production of outputs to the stewarding of generated outputs. It does not spend much attention on contemplative forms of critical thinking, the development of a worker’s understanding, other than to emphasize that that form of CT is an important and essential facet for the productive side of work. This is alarming, though: in the list of cognitive activities and how knowledge workers perceived a decrease in critical thinking, the highest number of “less effort” or “much less effort”’s reported were for comprehension. “In the majority of examples, knowledge workers perceive decreased effort for cognitive activities associated with critical thinking when using GenAI compared to not using one — examples that were reported as “much less effort” or “less effort” comprise 72% in Knowledge, <b><i><u>79% in Comprehension,</u></i></b> 69% in Application, 72% in Analysis, 76% in Synthesis, and 55% in Evaluation dataset” (5.1). So the area where the most people are reporting a decrease in cognitive activity, letting AI do the work, is COMPREHENSION. 
“Moreover, while GenAI can improve worker efficiency, it can inhibit critical engagement with work and can potentially lead to long-term overreliance on the tool and diminished skill for independent problem-solving. Higher confidence in GenAI’s ability to perform a task is related to less critical thinking effort. <u>When using GenAI tools, the effort invested in critical thinking shifts from information gathering to information verification; from problem-solving to AI response integration; and from task execution to task stewardship</u>”(7.0). There’s a huge focus in this paper on those three categories: information gathering/verification, problem solving/response integration, and task execution/task stewardship, and it skips over a critical step in between: developing understanding. In the GenAI-less workflow, information gathering is the basis for **deep understanding**, which then acts as the foundation for problem solving and task execution. If the GenAI-powered workflow centers on processing initial information produced into a problem-solver, then integrating that response into a task-completer, the opportunities for working with the subject at hand oneself are decimated. 
Think of the knowledge at hand as clay. Instead of the sculptor gathering mud from the earth, treating the mud to transform it into the proper sort of clay with added sands and dyes, then sculpting that clay and firing it themselves, the “sculptor” becomes a human conveyor belt, moving material from the clay-gathering widget to the processing widget, then from the processing widget into the sculpting widget. The “sculptor” has given up control over most of the stages of his own workflow, and if he trusts the machine he has given the work over to, he is unlikely to perform a great detail of critical evaluation of the machine’s outputs. A passive, trusting “sculptor” relinquishes control to the machines and skips the chance at comprehension of the particular piece, but remains responsible for the produced sculpture. Over time, it’s reasonable to assume that this sculptor’s understanding of his own craft will be eroded. The fundamentals will remain strong, but specific and situational nuances and expertise will diminish because the opportunities to flex those muscles are diminished.
