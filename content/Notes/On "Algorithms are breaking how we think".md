The key term is “algorithmic complacency” - the willingness to relinquish control/decision making to algorithms

The very concept of a “For You” page is an indication of algorithmic complacency 

“Algorithmic complacency, if not noticed and acted upon, means you’re allowing other people, who are not you, to decide what matters to you.”

“it’s one thing to automate an elevator, but a very different thing to automate what people see”   

Automated curation is subtle and risky.

The disconnect between the user’s definition of “best results” and the algorithmic definition of “best results” creates systematic flaws and opportunity for gaps.

“If I don’t vet what [a generative algorithm] is finding, then what I’m saying is that I don’t want to be responsible for what I do [with that information”
