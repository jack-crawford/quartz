#on #notes #tolkien [[Tolkien Notes]]
Unlike [[On Deor, Wulf and Eadwacer, and The Wife’s Lament]], Gormlaith’s Laments end on a high note. She runs through all of her trials, her suffering, her many loves lost, but in the end tells herself it is time to stop dwelling in grief and to move on!
There is a very moving section where she shouts at the monk burying her greatest love, Niall, and tells him to get away.
