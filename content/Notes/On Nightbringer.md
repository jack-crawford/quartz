
#notes #on 

This was the recommended entry point into the Warhammer book universe according to a long Reddit post that I found on Polygon ([https://www.polygon.com/warhammer-40k/522708/warhammer-40k-horus-heresy-reading-guide-cd8d-redditor](https://www.polygon.com/warhammer-40k/522708/warhammer-40k-horus-heresy-reading-guide-cd8d-redditor))

  

The book follows Uriel Ventris and Ariel Barzona, a captain of the Ultramarines and an Inquisitor, respectively, who are sent to a remote world, ostensibly to quell cultish insurrection and get the tithing numbers back up to snuff. 

Instead they find a complex conspiracy driven by one of the world’s factory-cartel leaders, who has aligned himself with some sinister aliens to resurrect an ancient godlike death beast. 

  

This is a book chockablock with violence and gore, where the heroes are the Empire’s enforcers and the villains are pain and death incarnate. It was pretty good, though I found Barzona to be the most interesting character and he dies near the end, which makes reading many more of these books an unlikely future for me.

  

There are 60+ books in this series, interwoven through the different factions and forces of the Warhammer universe, but perhaps at some point I’ll just read the rest of the recommended introductory path (continuing with _Storm of Iron_ and _Warriors of Ultramar_)
