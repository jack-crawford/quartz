# Where am I?
This is my public digital garden, a place for the best things from my private digital garden to be displayed in a more curated, intentional place. Writing, notes, observations, anything that I think has enough merit and coherence will end up here. If my private garden is a wild, overgrown backyard teeming with weeds and half-finished projects, this is the pruned and raked front yard. 
# What is the point of this?
To share my notes and thoughts in a systemic, non-linear flow. [[On Digital Gardens|Here are my notes on the subject]], which plenty of brilliant people have discussed over the years. Instead of a linear stream of writing and content like a traditional blog or newsletter, digital gardens operate across multiple axes. [They're rhizomatic, not arborescent.](https://jzhao.xyz/thoughts/rhizomatic-vs-arborescent) It's sort of a Zettelkasten, sort of a second brain, but one polished and available for those not at my desk. 
# How does this whole thing work?
I'm using a customized version of [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) to publish an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) vault to GitHub Pages with some formatting and experience tweaks. Navigate via the search, the sidebars, or the tags!
